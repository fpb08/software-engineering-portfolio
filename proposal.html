Portfolio Project Proposal

Project title

My Software Engineering Portfolio

Website purpose

The purpose of my website is to create a personal portfolio that showcases my programming skills, projects, and growth as an aspiring software engineer. I am interested in technology, artificial intelligence, and developing my own software and programs. This website will allow me to present the projects I have worked on, demonstrate my technical abilities, and share my future career goals. I also want the portfolio to show how I use problem-solving, creativity, and critical thinking to approach programming challenges. Ultimately, my goal is to build a professional online presence that can help me pursue software engineering internships and future career opportunities.

Target audience

  • Software engineering recruiters and hiring managers.
  • Companies offering software development internships.
  • Experienced developers reviewing my work.
  • College programs or other opportunities interested in my technical abilities.

Research findings about portfolio requirements

  • Source 1: UC Davis Career Center
    Source: UC Davis Career Center — Portfolios
    Research finding: Professional portfolios can showcase a person's accomplishments, abilities, and work samples. Organizing relevant work and providing clear information helps visitors understand the creator's qualifications.
  • Source 2: University of Florida Career Connections Center
    Source: University of Florida — Portfolio Foundations: Building Your Portfolio
    Research finding: A professional portfolio can include an introduction, relevant projects, and a résumé. These elements help present a person's background and demonstrate their qualifications to potential employers.

My research showed me that a professional portfolio should do more than introduce a person. It should also demonstrate their abilities through relevant projects and provide information about their qualifications. The UC Davis Career Center emphasizes showcasing accomplishments and work samples, while the University of Florida Career Connections Center provides guidance on organizing portfolio content, including projects and a résumé. Based on these findings, I plan to include separate pages for my background, projects, technical skills, résumé, and contact information. This structure will make it easier for potential employers to understand my abilities and explore my work.

Planned website components

  • Home: Gives visitors a quick overview of who I am and what I do.
  • About Me: Helps employers understand my interests, motivation, and direction.
  • Projects: Demonstrates my coding experience and problem-solving abilities.
  • Skills: Helps employers identify my technical strengths and areas of experience.
  • Resume: Allows potential employers to review my qualifications.
  • Contact: Gives recruiters and other visitors a way to contact me.

External references

  1. University of California, Davis Career Center. Portfolios. https://careercenter.ucdavis.edu/resumes-and-materials/portfolios
  2. University of Florida Career Connections Center. Portfolio Foundations: Building Your Portfolio. https://careerhub.ufl.edu/resources/portfolio-foundations-building-your-portfolio/
